Every Monday, teams celebrate green stages and full calendars. By Friday, the same deals sit in the same stage with new excuses attached.

The trap is measuring **activity** instead of **evidence of decision progress**.

Before you accept a forecast commit, ask one question:

> *What changed in the buyer's process since last week—not in our CRM notes, but in their org?*

If the answer is “nothing,” the deal is not advancing. It is aging.

Three quick signals that a deal is real:

1. **New stakeholder entered** with a defined concern (not a courtesy intro)
2. **Mutual action item** with a customer-owned deadline
3. **Document or workshop scheduled** that produces an internal artifact

Skip the pipeline theater. Track decision movement. Your quarter will thank you.
